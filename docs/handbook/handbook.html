How to use this book

Three readers, one product

Chapter 1 of 49+10 XP

ClubPro is used by three kinds of people, and this book has a part for each. You do not need to read it all: members need Part B, office bearers Parts B and C, the operator Part D.

Members

Apply, sign in, keep the e-card, register for events, borrow books, pay dues, vote. Mostly on a phone. Part B

Office bearers

Secretary, president, treasurer, librarian and the administrator: approvals, publishing, minutes, the library desk, accounts, broadcasts, settings. Usually on a laptop. Part C

The operator

Team Bpro, or whoever runs the server: installing, creating clubs, pricing, billing, channels, backups. Part D

Each chapter opens with At a glance, then numbered steps with the screen beside them. Symbols:

💡
Tip — a shortcut or a good habit.
⚠️
Careful — something that cannot be undone, or that members will notice.
ℹ️
Note — background that explains why the screen behaves as it does.
🗣️
Malayalam — the Malayalam name of the screen or button, as members see it.
🎓Level 1 · Member🏅Level 2 · Office bearer🛠️Level 3 · Operator📚Reference
Why levels?Each part is a level. Finish a level's missions in the app and you have earned its badge. No account on earth is checking; it is a way to make sure you tried every screen once.

Languages, phones and the installed app

Malayalam and English everywhere

Chapter 3 of 49+10 XP
At a glance
  • Every screen, button and message exists in English and Malayalam.
  • The language button sits at the top of every page; a member's choice is remembered and used for the messages the club sends them.
  • On a phone the app installs to the home screen and opens full-screen, with notifications.
  1. Open the club's web address in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).

ℹ️
The app keeps working on a weak connection: screens you have opened before load instantly, and a clear You are offline page appears instead of an error.

Applying for membership

Six steps, two minutes

Chapter 5 of 49+10 XP
At a glance
  • The form saves itself as you go; a refresh loses nothing.
  • Family members can be added now or later from your profile.
  • The photo can be a selfie or a picture from the gallery; it becomes your e-card photo.
  1. Tap Apply for membership on the front page.
  2. About you name in English and, if you like, Malayalam; gender, date of birth, blood group. The blood group lets the club find donors in an emergency.
  3. Contact and address your mobile becomes your login. WhatsApp number if different, e-mail, house name, ward, pincode.
  4. Family tap a chip (spouse, son, daughter, father, mother) and fill the name and date of birth. Family members can be registered for events and competitions.
  5. Photo Take a selfie opens the camera; Choose from gallery picks a file. It is resized on the phone before upload.
  6. Password and consent 8 characters or more; confirm that the details are true and that the club may message you.
  7. Review and send check everything, then Send application. A What happens next page confirms it.
Selfie or galleryStep 4 offers both: Take a selfie opens the camera; Choose from gallery picks any picture. The photo is shrunk on the phone before it is sent, so it works on slow connections.
Did you know?The form keeps a draft in the phone's browser. Close the browser, come back tomorrow, and the steps you filled are still there until you send or clear them.
Secretary's tipAsk the applicant to write the name in English exactly as on their ID. It goes on the e-card and the receipts.
+20 XPMission 2 · Apply
  • Fill all six steps on a phone, including one family member
  • Take a selfie for the photo
  • Send the application and read the What happens next page
ℹ️
If the office rejects an application the applicant is told why by message and can apply again with corrections.

Signing in and forgotten passwords

Chapter 6 of 49+10 XP
  1. Sign in with the phone number used in the application (or the e-mail the office gave you) and your password.
  2. Forgot password? tap the link under the sign-in form, enter your phone or e-mail, and a 6-digit code arrives by WhatsApp, SMS or e-mail.

Paying dues and fees

UPI, online, or at the desk

Chapter 12 of 49+10 XP
At a glance
  • My payments shows what is due this year and every receipt the club has issued you.
  • Pay by UPI: scan or tap, then tell the club the transaction number; the treasurer confirms and the receipt arrives.
  • Where the club has online payment, Pay online settles it instantly with an automatic receipt.
Member's tipWrite the 12-digit UTR, not the amount. The treasurer matches it in the bank app in seconds.
  1. Open My payments the amount due and the items it covers are at the top.
  2. Pay by UPI scan the QR with Google Pay, PhonePe or any UPI app, or tap Open UPI app.
  3. I have paid enter the 12-digit UTR or transaction ID from your UPI app.
  4. Receipt the treasurer confirms the payment; your receipt arrives by WhatsApp or e-mail and stays under My payments.
ℹ️
Paying at the office works as before: the treasurer records it and the receipt is messaged to you.
Did you know?Receipts are never deleted. If one is wrong it is reversed with a voucher, so what you were messaged always matches the club's cash book.

First-time setup

An hour, once

Chapter 16 of 49+10 XP
At a glance
  • Settings holds the club's identity, numbering, membership types, library rules, website, branding and channels.
  • Users & roles gives office bearers their sign-in.
  • Import the old register from Excel; members then sign in with their phone number.
Do this firstDo Settings before you import the register: the short name and the number format are baked into every membership number you issue.
  1. Club names in both scripts, short name, registration number, address, phone, e-mail. Tick Allow self-registration if people may apply online. List the wards; enter the club's UPI ID so members can pay.
  2. Numbering membership, receipt, voucher and accession number formats with placeholders ({prefix} {year} {yy} {seq:04d}); whether the sequence restarts each year; subscription due month.
  3. Membership types &amp; fees life, ordinary, associate, student, honorary — or your own, with admission and annual fees in rupees. These drive dues.
  4. Library loan period, books per member, fine per day, online renewals; event reminder days.
  5. Website about text in both languages, map link, social links and a cover photo for the public website.
  6. Branding logo, primary and accent colour, app name, footer credit (chapter 44 explains).
  7. Communication channel status and, if the club has its own WhatsApp / SMS / e-mail / Razorpay accounts, their keys.
  8. Save the button at the bottom saves every card at once.
💡
Give every office bearer their own login. The audit log then shows who approved, published or recorded what.

Publishing news, events and competitions

One post, every channel

Chapter 18 of 49+10 XP
At a glance
  • A post can be news, an event, a camp, a social activity or a competition, with dates, venue, fee and registration.
  • Publish and notify sends it to the chosen audience by WhatsApp, SMS, e-mail and push in one click.

  1. New post (+) choose the kind; fill the title and body in English and Malayalam (the member's language is used).
  2. Registration tick Registration open, set a limit and a fee if any; family members may be allowed.
  3. Publish and notify pick the audience (all members, a type, a ward, a committee, blood group…) and the channels; the outbox does the rest.

  6. Results enter or publish results; members are notified and the public page shows them.
Did you know?Publish and notify writes one outbox row per member per channel. For 300 members on WhatsApp, SMS and app that is 900 rows, sent in the background while you carry on working.
Keep WhatsApp preciousPublish news without notification for small updates; keep WhatsApp for things members must act on. Members notice the difference.

Governance without paper

Part C · Office

The Library Council pack

The affiliation return, a grade estimate, grants and utilisation certificates

Chapter 21 of 49+10 XP
At a glance
  • Library → Library Council reads the year's affiliation statement straight from the registers you already keep: stock, additions, members, circulation, reading room, newspapers, programmes, library accounts, grants and office bearers.
  • A grade estimate scores the library against thresholds kept in Settings and shows, per criterion, what the next grade needs.
  • Grants have a register; the vouchers paid from a grant are charged to it and the utilisation certificate prints with the certificate text for signature.
  1. Settings first under Settings → Library Council affiliation enter the Council affiliation number, the grade the Council gave, district, taluk, working hours and the librarian; check the grade thresholds against the Council's current circular.
  2. Grade estimate each criterion (stock, members, books issued, periodicals, reading-room visits, programmes, books added) earns points by the grade band it reaches; the overall estimate follows the points. The right-hand column says what the next band needs.
  3. Annual statement the printable return for the year, in English or Malayalam, with signature lines for the librarian, secretary and president.
  4. Grants record a grant when it arrives: the receipt is booked under Grant for you. Open Vouchers charged to tick the payment vouchers spent from it.
  5. Utilisation certificate prints the grant, the vouchers charged, the unutilised balance and the certificate wording.
⚠️
The thresholds shipped with the app are planning targets, not the Council's figures. Replace them in Settings with the numbers in the current circular; the screens and the printout both say the circular governs.
Librarian's tipKeep the reading-room register and the periodicals register up to date through the year: two of the seven criteria come straight from them.
+30 XPBonus mission · Affiliation
  • Enter the Council details and the current thresholds in Settings
  • Record a grant and charge two vouchers to it
  • Print the annual statement in Malayalam

Accounts, dues and payments

Receipts, vouchers, UPI and online

Chapter 22 of 49+10 XP
At a glance
  • Every rupee in or out is a receipt or a voucher under a standard head (admission, subscription, donation, event fee, rent, salary…).
  • Dues are computed from membership types and the subscription year; reminders go automatically.

Statements, cash book and the year close

Receipts & Payments, Income & Expenditure, Balance Sheet, Tally

Chapter 23 of 49+10 XP
At a glance
  • Accounts → Statements turns the receipts and vouchers into the three statements an auditor and the society's annual return expect, for a financial year (April to March unless Settings say otherwise).
  • Every head has a nature: income and expense meet in Income & Expenditure; books, furniture and building are assets; deposits are liabilities; building fund and corpus are funds. The balance sheet balances by construction.

  4. Statements the three statements for any year, provisional until the year is closed; Print statements gives the bilingual set with signature lines for treasurer, secretary, president and auditor.
  5. Close the year after the audit, from the Statements screen (treasurer or administrator). Reopen if the auditor asks for a change; close again afterwards.

Part D · Operator

How the platform is built

One server, many clubs

Chapter 31 of 49+10 XP

ClubPro is a single Python application (FastAPI) with one SQLite database per club, a browser app that installs on phones, and background workers for messages, daily jobs and backups. Caddy in front provides HTTPS for every club's domain automatically. The first club on a server is the default club and lives directly in the data folder; every further club lives under data/tenants/<slug>/. The visitor's domain picks the club; the operator's own pages (/signup, /pricing, /_platform) answer on any domain.

Part D · Operator

Installing on a server

A VPS, a domain, fifteen minutes

Chapter 32 of 49+10 XP
  1. Get a VPS Ubuntu or Debian, 1 vCPU / 2 GB is enough for dozens of clubs. Shared web hosting cannot run it (it is Python, not PHP).
  2. Point DNS an A record for the platform domain (e.g. clubpro.in) and a wildcard record (*.clubpro.in) at the server; clubs with their own domains point theirs the same way.
  3. Run the installer curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BABUKOLLAM/bpro-club-manager/main/deploy/vps-install.sh | sudo bash -s -- clubpro.in admin@teambpro.in installs Docker and Caddy, clones the app to /opt/club, writes .env and starts everything.
  4. Multi-club Caddy replace the site file with deploy/site-multi.caddy so certificates are issued on demand for every registered domain.
  5. Set the operator keys in /opt/club/.env: CLUB_MASTER_KEY, CLUB_BASE_DOMAIN=clubpro.in, CLUBPRO_RZP_*, and the server's SMS / WhatsApp / SMTP settings used for signup codes and as the default for clubs. Restart with docker compose up -d.
  6. Open the console https://clubpro.in/_platform with the master key: set the product name, the footer credit, pricing, your UPI ID and support contact.
💡
Re-running the installer command updates the app in place. Data, uploads and .env are kept.
Operator's ruleKeep .env out of git and out of WhatsApp. It holds every key. Back it up with the server, nowhere else.

Can two clubs share one phone number for WhatsApp?
Yes: leave the club's WhatsApp fields blank and the server's number is used. A club that enters its own number uses that instead.
We already have 400 members in Excel.
Office → Members → Import register: download the template, paste your rows, upload. Existing numbers are kept; missing ones are issued.
Can a member belong to two clubs?
Yes. Each club is separate; the member signs in to each at its own address, with the same phone number.
What if we stop paying ClubPro?
After the end date and 15 days of grace the club becomes read-only: everyone can still sign in, read and export; nothing is deleted. Paying reopens it at once.
Is our data safe?
Each club has its own database on the server with nightly backups, optionally copied to Google Drive. Passwords are hashed; every change is logged; uploads are re-encoded; public endpoints are rate-limited.
Can we use our own domain?
Yes: point the domain at the server and add it on the platform console (or ask the operator). The certificate is issued automatically on the first visit.
Can a residents' association use ClubPro?
Yes. Settings → This organisation is a… → association. Part E covers units, maintenance billing and the helpdesk; everything else works as for a club.
Our auditor wants Receipts & Payments and a Balance Sheet.
Accounts → Statements, for any financial year, printable with signature lines; enter opening balances once and close the year after the audit (chapter 23).
How do we get help?
The support contact set by the operator appears under Subscription plan and in reminder messages. The short in-app handbook is under Help in the menu.
